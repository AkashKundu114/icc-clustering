# Support and Inquiries

Thank you for your interest in the Inductive Consensus Clustering (`icc`) codebase and research framework.

---

## 1. How to Get Help

- **Documentation:**
  - [API Reference](docs/api.md)
  - [Theoretical Foundations](docs/THEORETICAL_FOUNDATIONS.md)
  - [Remediation & Empirical Audit Ledger](governance/remediation/LEDGER.md)

- **Bug Reports & Issues:**
  If you find a bug, unexpected behaviour, or a numerical inconsistency:
  - Check the [Issue Tracker](https://github.com/AkashKundu114/icc-clustering/issues) to see if it has already been reported.
  - File a new issue using our issue templates with a minimal reproducible code snippet, input data shape, seed, and your environment specifications (`python -V`, `pip freeze`).

- **Reproducibility Inquiries:**
  If you encounter discrepancies reproducing the benchmark tables or figures:
  - Consult the [Reproduction Guide](README.md#reproducing-the-paper).
  - Verify that your environment flags match the single-thread determinism constraints (`OMP_NUM_THREADS=1`, `MKL_NUM_THREADS=1`).
