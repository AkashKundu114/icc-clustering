# Nine-cell subset discrepancy

The stored Boolean flag marks **20** rows: Rule (i), stability ≥0.50 and mean ARI ≤0.05. It is not the nine-cell selection. Recovered `audit_degeneracy.py` applies the later Rule (i′): a cell is nondegenerate when at least 27/30 repeats avoid either observed K below target K or minimum cluster fraction below 5%. Applying this post-hoc screen to the 20 Rule (i) candidates excludes CoreHalo–Prostate (one cluster throughout) and CoHiRF–Prostate (18/30 degenerate), leaving **18**.

The nine manuscript cells are exactly the Rule (i′) cells for methods FCAG, FCM, and FastMVC, each qualifying on three cohorts (Rule iii recurrence). They are a further selected subset of 18; this subset can be reconstructed from method recurrence, but Rule (i′) was added after observing CoreHalo’s prostate collapse. No evidence establishes the criteria as preregistered.

Nine-cell values: mean stability 0.6837644; mean ARI −0.0028530; mean gap 0.6866174; median gap 0.6661357. Rule (i) 20-cell mean gap=0.7191491, median=0.7004957. Full 72 gap=0.4642739, median=0.4484068. The exact 18 qualifying cells and nine are marked in `subset_reconstruction.csv`.

**Decision: QUALIFY, not headline evidence.** Retain only as post-hoc, criterion-conditioned descriptive analysis, alongside all 18 and the full 72. It is not independent confirmation. If the manuscript presents nine cells as preregistered or as confirmation, remove that framing.
