# Proposed data availability statement — interim, not applied

> The six cohort datasets were obtained from their upstream sources as documented in the accompanying dataset provenance table. The public repository does not currently contain the complete recovered feature caches, saved subsample partitions, per-fit records, or all raw benchmark score records used in the analyses. These files have not yet been deposited in a verified, publicly accessible, rights-cleared archive. Consequently, aggregate tables can be checked from the repository, but independent partition-level or full-experiment reproduction is not currently available from a fresh clone. Dataset-specific access conditions and redistribution permissions must be checked at the upstream source; no third-party data are redistributed here.

Do not publish as final until source-specific licenses, acquisition versions, and a rights-cleared archive have been reviewed.
