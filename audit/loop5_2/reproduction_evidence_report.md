## 3. Explanatory Statement on Benchmark Rerun Scope

The complete 3,000-fit omnibus benchmark cannot be re-executed from scratch within this audit turn for three verifiable technical reasons:
1. **Computational Budget:** Re-fitting 12 clustering algorithms across 13 datasets and 30 seeds would require an estimated 380 to 450 CPU-hours (several days of continuous computation).
2. **Missing Raw Baseline Scripts:** As identified in Task 2, original standalone generation scripts for external baselines `CoHiRF` and `CoreHalo` are not packaged as turnkey scripts in `scripts/`.
3. **Absence of 30-Seed Partition Arrays:** The primary subsample partition labels for seeds 11–30 on the high-D cohorts are not present on local disk.

All headline claims that rely on existing artifacts have been fully, independently verified and reconciled.
