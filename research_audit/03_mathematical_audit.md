# Mathematical audit (limited: no manuscript)
BC formula in code: (g^2+1)/(excess_kurt + 3(N-1)^2/((N-2)(N-3)) + 1e-5): matches finite-sample SBC; module docstring (kurtosis+3) differs from code. Pearson kurtosis >= skew^2+1, equality iff two-point -> BC<=1, =1 for two-point/sparse-binary features. Gaussian null BC ~1/3 (measured mean 0.337, max 0.406 at N=500), threshold 5/9.
Bipartite step: B_norm=B/rowsum; W=B_norm^T B_norm; degree = column sums of B_norm (consistent with transfer-cut with D_X=I). Embedding U=B_norm V (smallest-k eigvecs of L_norm, no D_L^-1/2, no eigenvalue scaling, includes trivial vector) is NOT shown equivalent to full spectral on S: STRONG CONCERN. Complexity: N<=2000 builds N x N S (O(N^2)). Beyer et al. concentration does not imply identical partitions; no theorem in repo.

## A3 numerical results (scripts/a3_embedding.py, a3_paths.py)
Embedding: on 3 synthetic ensembles (rebuilt co-association, not the model's exact B), the current embedding B_norm V spans the same top-k subspace as the dense graph B_norm D_L^-1 B_norm^T (principal-angle cosines 1.000 to 3 d.p.), so the "same eigenspace" claim holds approximately there. It lacks the D_L^-1/2 and sigma^-1/2 scaling, so k-means outputs differ from dense spectral (student_t: ARI 0.887 legacy vs 0.486 transfer-cut = dense). Opt-in `embedding='transfer_cut'` implemented; on 8 real datasets mean ARI 0.158 vs 0.167 (no benefit). Verdict: "exact spectral extraction" is not established at clustering level; the legacy embedding is a different (not worse) algorithm.
