## Dataset-specific cautions

- OpenML's [Terms of Use](https://docs.openml.org/intro/terms/) require uploaders to affirm that they have the rights and permissions needed to reproduce and publish submitted content; downstream use is subject to any licenses granted by the uploader. OpenML's [dataset metadata documentation](https://docs.openml.org/data/) makes the uploader provide a `licence`, and its [server schema](https://openml.github.io/server-api/development/database/openml_expdb/) describes that field as the license under which the dataset is shared (with `Public` as its default/example). The six exact biomedical records expose `licence=Public`, but that metadata alone does not establish that the uploader had authority over the underlying third-party cohort or clear the exact derivative and its sample-level outputs. Those variants therefore remain REDISTRIBUTION_NOT_CLEARED until source-specific terms, lineage, and any needed permission are confirmed.
- Canonical UCI pages for ISOLET, 20 Newsgroups, Sonar, and Madelon report CC BY 4.0, but the project uses OpenML/scikit-learn variants or a selected subset. The canonical terms do not automatically establish permissions for those exact variants.
- The 10x Fresh 68k PBMC Donor A source states CC BY 4.0; the Scanpy `pbmc68k_reduced` page documents a processed, sampled derivative but does not state a separate dataset license. Match the local cache to that exact derivative and retain upstream and software attribution before considering redistribution.
- The scikit-learn Newsgroups representation is a filtered/processed variant of user-authored messages. The canonical UCI CC BY 4.0 record is relevant but does not by itself resolve all terms for the exact fetched representation or its sample-level outputs.
- The project registry points to the EHU corrected 200-band Indian Pines file. Purdue’s CC0 record concerns a distinct 220-band item; do not transfer its license to the EHU variant.
- Yale’s official page permits research use with acknowledgment but requires permission to include images in a publicly distributed larger database. Do not redistribute images or derived sample-level artifacts absent permission.
- The registry’s `Zheng68K` entry uses Scanpy’s `pbmc68k_reduced` example (700 samples × 765 features), not the full 68,579-cell Zheng matrix. The names refer to related source family, not identical feature matrices; exact lineage and rights remain unresolved.

For any use, acquire data from the linked official provider, follow its current terms, cite the exact source/version and underlying study, record retrieval date and local checksums, and do not share uncleared transformed or sample-level artifacts.
