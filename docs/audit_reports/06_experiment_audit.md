## E3 pool-size sensitivity (experiments/audit_controls/src/e3_pool_curve.py; experiments/audit_controls/data/e3_pool_curve.csv; 8 cached datasets, 5 seeds, BC-ranked vs random pool)
Mean ARI over datasets by pool fraction (2/5/10/15/25/40/100%): BC-ranked 0.123/0.143/0.191/0.168/0.060/0.049/0.042; random 0.033/0.036/0.030/0.029/0.033/0.033/n.a.
SRBCT: BC 0.098/0.557/0.550/0.604/0.528/0.306/0.074 (random ~0.07-0.12). Leukemia: BC 0.737/0.483/0.696/0.631/-0.057/0.054/0.228 (random 0.12-0.20). Colon: BC 0.105/0.100/0.139/0.061/-0.010/-0.009 (random ~0). CNS, Prostate, Ovarian, Sonar, Madelon: ~0 at every size.
Reading: (1) BC ranking carries real signal on SRBCT/Leukemia/(Colon) beyond a random pool; (2) the benefit lives in a window (about 5-15%) and collapses outside it (Leukemia at 25%, SRBCT at 2% and >=40%); (3) the legacy 15% floor sits inside the window but nothing label-free selects it, and choosing the best fraction from these curves would be label-tuned (circular); (4) 5 of 8 datasets show no effect at any size. Caveat: 5 seeds, high variance (Leukemia SD ~0.27 in earlier 10-seed runs).
