Reading: (1) BC ranking carries real signal on SRBCT/Leukemia/(Colon) beyond a random pool; (2) the benefit lives in a window (about 5-15%) and collapses outside it (Leukemia at 25%, SRBCT at 2% and >=40%); (3) the legacy 15% floor sits inside the window but nothing label-free selects it, and choosing the best fraction from these curves would be label-tuned (circular); (4) 5 of 8 datasets show no effect at any size. Caveat: 5 seeds, high variance (Leukemia SD ~0.27 in earlier 10-seed runs).

## E3b label-free pool-size selection (experiments/audit_controls/src/e3b_label_free_selector.py; experiments/audit_controls/data/e3b_selector.csv) -- NEGATIVE RESULT
Pre-specified before running: grid {2,5,10,15,25,40}% BC pool; criteria = silhouette of labels in U_emb, and mean sample stability; selection without labels; 8 datasets x 5 seeds.
Mean ARI: fixed 15% 0.168; grid-mean 0.122; selected-by-silhouette 0.091; selected-by-stability 0.091; oracle-best (label-peeking upper bound) 0.240. Leukemia: fixed15 0.631 vs 0.172/0.102 selected; SRBCT: stability-selected 0.604 (= fixed15), silhouette 0.516. Median chosen fraction 0.15 (sil), 0.125 (stab).
Conclusion: neither criterion recovers a good pool size; both fall below even the grid mean. No further criteria were tried (to avoid criterion-shopping on 8 datasets). Whether the 15% default itself was tuned using labels is UNVERIFIED. Any future selector needs a declared search budget and evaluation on held-out datasets.
